The market failure · and the person standing in it

Where bank sanctions screening starts — and the vendors will not sell it to a 12-person NGO at any price.

Amara runs grants at a humanitarian NGO moving money toward three sanctioned regions. The same strict-liability statute binds her and JPMorgan: intent is irrelevant, headcount is irrelevant. The morning Treasury updates the SDN list, every queued disbursement — grantees, field partners, vendors — becomes her personal federal exposure.

A true hit must be held and reported within 10 business days. A delisting means blocked funds must be released. Today she does it by hand, late, or not at all.

02 · problem
strict liability · every US person · headcount is irrelevant
Sanctions compliance is a solved problem — if you're a bank. The statute is strict liability: intent doesn't matter, a wrong payment is a violation. The tooling market does not serve small organisations at all. The delta on screen is real: Aug 7, 18 additions, 8 removals, and we hold its SHA-256. Both directions matter — additions freeze money she was about to send, removals free money she has been blocking.

Architecture · three agents · two planes · one guard

Deterministic code decides what executes.

Three agents, one process. The separation is enforced by the Adjudicator protocol and the guard, not by a network hop — calling this a fleet of microservices would be a nicer diagram and a false one.
04 · architecture
Gemini explains and judges — deterministic code decides what executes
The FEF sub-question asks how the system recovers when a worker loops or hallucinates. Because every model call is confined to one module, the oracle guard on the return path is a real check rather than a formality — and it fails closed, so a confused agent can never move money. Note the honesty line at the bottom: one process, not microservices. The fully detailed drawn architecture, with the file that implements every block, is in the repo.

Autonomy, graded · no self-grading anywhere

Four decisions, four external graders.

HOLD money stops
An adjudicated true hit parks the counterparty and every queued disbursement — idempotent by database constraint, so a re-screen cannot double-hold.
The SDN record on treasury.gov — name, DOB, passport. make challenge reproduces it for any name you type.
CLEAR payment proceeds
The disbursement proceeds with the reason on record — the signal breakdown that ended it, using the 4,393 OFAC-flagged weak aliases in the 08/07/2026 publication.
OpenSanctions yente, scope-pinned to us_ofac_sdn and consulted on every adjudication, not only where it agrees. An oracle consulted selectively is not an oracle.
RELEASEmoney moves again
A delisting retires the hold and the money moves again, without anyone noticing the delta first.
Treasury's own published delta (/changes/latest) and the Federal Register notice. Today this leg runs as a labelled REPLAY of the real 2026-08-07 removals — it says REPLAY on every screen and is never implied live.
REPORTstatutory artifact
Drafts the blocking report against the real 10-business-day clock and files it to the append-only ledger.
The federal calendar, 5 U.S.C. 6103, and the public SDN record. Transmission to OFAC stays human — we draft and file, we do not submit.
07 · decisions
each decision executes without a human click — and each is graded by something we do not control
The design rule: no capability is judged by us. Holds grade against Treasury's record, clears against an independent screening engine, releases against Treasury's own delta and the Federal Register, reports against the statutory calendar. The 4,393 weak aliases are real feed data — that is what makes false-positive clearing demonstrable rather than staged. And say the last line plainly: we do not transmit to OFAC, and a system that claimed to would be lying.
Fortified · what the track actually asks: what happens when a worker lies, loops, or dies

A confused agent cannot move money.

The guard does not trust the answer

“The counterparty name AL AZZAWI HIKMAT MISBAN IBRAHIM closely matches the primary name Hikmat Mizban Ibrahim AL-AZZAWI on the SDN record, with full to…” gemini-3.5-flash-lite · adjudication 59 · uid 7870 · 0.9283 · HOLD · guard AGREE · yente HIT 7870 0.900 · 1 round trip
Every cited identifier is checked against the record — a fabricated alias in a federal blocking report is the worst output this system could produce. Also refused: a CLEAR on a near-identical name with no contradicting evidence, a HOLD below the no-hit floor, a rationale too thin to file. On refusal it asks once more with the disagreement stated, then stops. The cap is two round trips — in code, and again as a database CHECK.

“Wrong” and “did not answer” are different failures

They were the same failure here until the first real Gemini run quarantined 438 of 536 counterparties — not one of them a bad verdict. The free tier allows five requests a minute; everything after the twenty-first returned 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED into a bare except. Quarantine is where a human is told the system could not safely decide; burying that one case in 438 destroys it.
  • PARSE_ERROR — the verdict cannot be trusted. No retry; a human reads it
  • ADJUDICATOR_UNAVAILABLE — the verdict was never produced. 5 attempts, honouring the server's own retry in Ns
  • Money moves in neither case. The difference is what the operator is told

This is the track criterion, answered with incidents rather than adjectives. The 438-quarantine story is the one to tell: it is a real bug we shipped, found, and fixed, and the fix is a distinction most agent systems never draw. The six tests on screen are the unattended-operation ones — kill a worker mid-book and resume at MIN(batch_start) over incomplete batches, not after the last completed one, because a scalar cursor would skip counterparties still in flight and report success.
Provenance · stated plainly, because a screening demo that blurs this is worthless

Sealed before Treasury moved.

make verify-book — verbatim stdout

The book is 400 names that were on the SDN list at seal time, chosen by the SHA-256 of each entry's uid — no RNG, byte-for-byte reproducible. It and its hash were committed on 2026-08-13, before any later OFAC publication existed. If Treasury delists one of them, git log proves the book predates the removal. As of 08/20, none has.

  • Treasury's data is unmodified. Every figure here is pinned to the 08/07/2026 publication — the live list is 19,249 records as of 08/20
  • The payment book is ours and synthetic, labelled by origin in the database, on every console screen, and on camera
  • The RELEASE leg is a labelled REPLAY of the real Aug-7 removals. It says REPLAY on screen and is never implied live
  • The graded run's ledger trigger reads MANUAL — a person typed the command. The chaining timer is armed in the plist and has not yet fired a re-screen
  • The 6-hourly poll was dead 08-17 to 08-22. A lint pass modernised timezone.utc under a Python 3.9 launchd invocation. Disclosed because “unattended” is a claim this project makes
  • Nothing runs on Google Cloud compute. Cloud Firestore's free tier holds the mirrored ledger; the agents, Postgres and yente are local
